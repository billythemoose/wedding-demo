Hotels

Hotel is the second easiest option and there are a ton of them around. Here are some options:

  • Davenport Hotel: The historic Davenport Hotel. Timeless elegance meets modern sophistication. It's also haunted.
  • Davenport Tower: The companion hotel, The Davenport Tower. Contemporary style, iconic guest service. It's literally across the street from the Davenport and it's safari themed.
  • Holiday Inn Express Spokane - Downtown: This hotel is on a big rock. Never stayed there but it's got that going for it.
  • DoubleTree by Hilton: Random other hotel option. Sounds like it'll be under construction while you're there which could be exciting. Maybe you can complain about the noise and get a discount on your stay.
  • Pear Shrub Motor Lodge: Don't stay here. It's near absolutely nothing and you might get shot.

Don't like those options? Figure it out yourself!

Your Parents' House

The best option, for better or worse, is staying at your parents' house. Why deal with having to pay for somewhere to stay and maybe somewhere to park when you can just crash at the family crib?

Pros:

  • You get to see your parents.
  • Your parents get to see you.
  • You might get free food.
  • There's probably a bag of coffee from 2005 in the freezer.
  • You can finally turn off Motion Smoothing on their TV.

Cons:

  • You have to see your parents.
  • Your bedroom might be a gym.
  • All the condiments are expired and you'll have to buy some more.

Your House

This is the ultra premium option. You already live there. Way to go, you!

Pros:

  • It's your house
  • Doesn't smell weird.
  • Might be a cat or a dog there.
  • Contains all your stuff.

Cons:

  • No free food.
