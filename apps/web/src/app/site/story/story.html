In the Beginning

Our paths first crossed in middle school band, under the strict tutelage of the one and only Mr. Henderson. Band class was a formative period for us both. Me, with utter disdain for concentration on classroom proceedings while sometimes playing the saxophone. Her, smacking the absolute shit out of random percussion instruments in the back of the room. Us both, thrilled to watch The Hunt for Red October for like the 3rd time during class cause it was Henderson's favorite movie or something. Honestly I don't quite remember where we actually met. Ask somebody else, they'll have a better idea. We've known each other since middle school and started dating sometime in college.

Life Continues

Man, so much has happened since then. Where to begin? I bought my first car, cute little Mazda Miata, its engine exploded, I had replaced under warranty, sold it. Bought car number two, a silver Honda Fit, $9,000, got rear-ended by a man named Justin Beaver (his legal name), totaled the car for $11,000. #Profit. Car number three, Toyota RAV4, nice and responsible, took it in for service, technicians put the engine together wrong, engine explodes, fixed under warranty, sold. Car number four, back to the Miata but a newer one, nothing bad happens, get bored, decide to drive from Seattle to Spokane on a random Friday to trade it in for an upgraded Miata RF, trade done in about 20 minutes. #success Now the car is paid off and it's about time for something new.

Other Things That Happened

Oh, did you not give a shit about the Annals of Car Ownership? Fine. Enjoy a stream of consciousness list of things that happened in no particular order:

  • We bought a house. Been in it for like 5 years. It's been a fun learning experience about how many things break and what it takes to fix them.
  • Took Jane on her first trips to new states and outside the country. Places we've gone: Banff, Maui, Costa Rica, Iceland, New Orleans, Montreal, the Oregon Coast, probably somewhere else I'm forgetting.
